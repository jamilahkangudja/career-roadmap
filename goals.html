Wait... What do I write for my goals? 🤔

For your assignment, you need three specific goals to finish by Week 8. Think of these as your "To-Do List" for your future career. Use the guide below to build your sentences.

Goal 1: Skill Development

The "Learning" Goal
1

What is this?

Pick a software or a certification you keep seeing in job descriptions. This is about getting better at a tool.

2

The Formula

"By Week 8, I will [Action Verb] [Tool Name] by completing [Specific Task]."

Copy this Example:

"By Week 8, I will master basic Excel formulas by completing the 'Excel Essential Training' on LinkedIn Learning."

Goal 2: Career Research

The "Investigation" Goal
1

What is this?

You have a major, but do you know which companies hire for it? This is about gathering data.

2

The Formula

"By Week 8, I will [Action Verb] [Target Job/Company] to find [Specific Information]."

Copy this Example:

"By Week 8, I will research five Marketing Agencies in Dallas to identify the starting salary and required GPA for their internships."

Goal 3: Networking

The "People" Goal
1

What is this?

Who is doing the job you want? This is about making a connection with a real person.

2

The Formula

"By Week 8, I will [Action Verb] [Person Type] on [Platform] to ask [1-2 Questions]."

Copy this Example:

"By Week 8, I will contact two ECU Alumni on LinkedIn to request a 15-minute phone call about their career path."

💡

Wait! Did you use an Action Verb?

Don't say "I want to..." or "I hope to...".
Start with: Complete, Research, Connect, Build, Finalize, or Analyze.
