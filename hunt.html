AI "Assist" Policy

🚦 Status: Yellow Light

AI is permitted for brainstorming, outlining, and refining your own ideas. However, submitting AI-generated content as your own final work is considered academic misconduct.

✅ DO USE FOR:

  • • Generating resume bullet point ideas
  • • Practicing interview responses
  • • Outlining professional emails

❌ DO NOT USE FOR:

  • • Copy-pasting entire essays
  • • Completing quizzes or exams
  • • Submitting without citation
